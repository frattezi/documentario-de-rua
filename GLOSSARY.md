# Documentário de Rua

The site of *Documentário de Rua*, an ongoing project that uses documentary filmmaking as a tool
for social transformation: telling the stories of people living on the street, turning visibility
into solidarity, and helping secure their rights. The site is both a showcase of the productions
and a place to take part in, support and fund them.

Terms here are the model, not the copy. Site copy is Brazilian Portuguese; these names are the
English identifiers used in code, data and docs.

## Language

**Project**:
*Documentário de Rua* itself: the continuing body of work and the people around it. The site
belongs to the Project, not to any single Film. The Project's name is the site's whole identity:
page title, Open Graph tags, byline, headings and the eventual domain all say *Documentário de
Rua*.
_Avoid_: the documentary, the channel, the site, Entre a Lei e a Rua (that is a Film)

**Film**:
A finished production of the Project, with its own title, synopsis, credits and screenings.
*Entre a Lei e a Rua* is the first one — Guilherme Simões's UFMG final-year film, which the
Project grew out of. A Film's title names only that Film: it never stands for the Project, and is
never translated or shortened.
_Avoid_: documentary, movie, video (a Film is not merely a video on the channel)

**Short**:
A vertical YouTube Shorts piece. A Story in its own right, never an excerpt standing in for a Film.
_Avoid_: clip, reel

**Story**:
Any single published piece in "histórias da rua" — a Film, a Short, or a social post. The unit the
channel section is fed with as new productions arrive.
_Avoid_: content, item, post

**Voice**:
A quoted testimony from someone who lives on the street, shown as a quote with its speaker and a
note. Voices are people, not sources.
_Avoid_: testimonial, quote, interviewee

**Supporter**:
Someone who gives money to the Project, via PIX.
_Avoid_: donor, patron

**Collaborator**:
Someone who offers work to the productions — filming, editing, or a story worth telling — through
the production form.
_Avoid_: volunteer, contributor (ambiguous with Supporter), crew

**Participant**:
Someone who joins the Project's actions and mobilisation without necessarily working on a
production, through the membership form.
_Avoid_: member, volunteer, follower
